Proposals and backend limits

Automatic naming and optional later rename are interaction proposals. Their persistence needs backend support. Current TEST has no rename operation. Local demo rename does not imply TEST support. Question revision/history here is illustrative. Evidence-grounded answers and synthesis stay disabled until agent access can be verified as restricted to the chosen sources.

Universal sources

Settings keeps one source model. Conversation and text-file selection are demonstrated; future web, database, repository, Topic and connected-service adapters are explicitly unavailable. No adapter operates in this preview. Manual collection is simulated, and saving configuration neither collects nor removes earlier copies. Files are bounded to the first 64 KiB; links do not gather their targets.

State and keyboard

Tab reaches actions, Enter/Space activates, and Escape closes the active inline editor/inspection/search before closing the Magnet. The puck moves with arrow keys or dragging on desktop. Settings Save and Cancel are distinct. New-question, per-Perspective revision and rename drafts remain separate when switching editors or questions. Cancel discards only the active draft. Draft, search, question history, inline inspection, collection feedback, position and reading place persist on reload. The surrounding workspace reader and draft are not changed. Reset affects only the demo. Focused browser checks do not constitute screen-reader certification.

Manage Brain — design round 2 (navigation refined)

One interaction design, shown in six states. Each page is full size (desktop 1440 × 900, phone 390 × 844) and opens in either theme. Drawn with Quiet Chat Mark 2's own stylesheets; nothing here is wired to a Brain. Mockup data

  1. The Brain icon and the Brain browserOne Brain icon on the existing rail (its dashes stay conversations) opens the Brain browser: search, Knowledge · Governing · Needs you · All, compact rows with Kind · status · plain words. Needs you stays in the global dock. darklight
  2. A record at restWhat it says, and whether it is in effect. Everything else is one click away. darklight
  3. The same record, details openedWhy it stands, how it has gone (delivered vs. used vs. helped), history — and the ⋯ menu holding every other action. darklight
  4. Your decision on a proposed ruleAdopt · Decline · Edit wording first, where it applies, and an optional reason. darklight
  5. A record beside the conversation it came fromOpened from a post's record mark; the conversation stays, the post stays marked; × closes it. darklight
  6. PhoneThe Brain list as a sheet over the conversation; a record as one screen with Back to the post. list, darklist, lightrecord, darkrecord, light
